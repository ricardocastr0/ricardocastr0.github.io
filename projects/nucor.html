Tools

During my Nucor internship, I worked on two systems: a desktop application for vibration analysis and a crane-orientation display attached to the main remote controller.

I developed the vibration-anomaly detector in .NET, using signal processing and regression to flag unusual vibration in industrial equipment.

For the crane display, I integrated an IMU, ESP32-S3, and LCD with a modular attachment to the remote controller. This work combined embedded sensing with the physical mounting and display interface.
