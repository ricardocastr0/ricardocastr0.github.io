Bigfoot

Designing and building a 1 m quasi-passive walking biped.

Role
Robotics Research Assistant
Timeline
Apr 2025 – Present
Scale
5× the starting lab design
Paper
Submitted · first author
Tools
MuJoCo, PD control, Raspberry Pi 5, CAN, CubeMars AK80-8, motion capture

I redesigned and built Bigfoot, scaling an existing lab biped design 5× to a 1 m platform in CMU’s Robomechanics Lab. My work covered mechanical design, simulation, control implementation, and hardware integration.

I explored designs in MuJoCo, implemented PD control, and integrated CubeMars AK80-8 actuators with a Raspberry Pi 5 over CAN. I also used motion capture during the project. The physical platform achieved walking.

I am the first author of a submitted conference paper on this work. This page will link to the paper when it is published.

This is a brief overview of my contribution. Detailed results and project media are reserved for the publication.
