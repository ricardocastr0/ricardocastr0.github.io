Robotic-hand mechanisms

Mechanical design and prototyping for a glove-controlled robotic hand.

Role
Mechanical Design Intern
Timeline
Aug 2025 – Jan 2026
Work
Mechanisms and teleoperation
Tools
Onshape, FeatureScript, potentiometers, servo actuation

I designed parametric mechanisms in Onshape and FeatureScript and prototyped fingers and linkages for a robotic hand. My work included both the mechanical parts and glove potentiometer-to-servo control.

The glove uses potentiometers to measure hand motion and provide inputs for the robotic hand’s servos. Working on the finger mechanisms alongside that input system gave me experience with the mechanical and control sides of teleoperation.

The project reported a 50% reduction in prototype mass and cost. The comparison baseline and method are not included in this public overview.

Project images are not shared publicly.
