CMU 24-688 · CAD and CAE

Hitch-mounted bike carrier

A folding two-bike rack developed as a CAD and structural-analysis project. It was not fabricated.

Timeline
Fall 2025
Team
Four students
My role
Concept lead, FEA, materials
CAD mass
80.2 → 27.6 kg
Costing scenario
45,000 units · planning only
Tools
Fusion 360, static stress FEA, material selection, manufacturing cost estimation

Our team designed a rack for a standard 2-inch receiver hitch that could carry two bikes and fold for storage. I worked on concept development, FEA, and materials. We selected a folding platform-tray design from four concepts, considering installation, bike-size compatibility, and storage space.

Static FEA identified a stress concentration at the hitch connection, so we added support prongs, reinforced the wheel-cradle arms, and kept steel at the hitch joint while switching the remaining members to aluminum. CAD-estimated mass fell from 80.2 to 27.6 kg (about 66%); these are model estimates, not weighed hardware.

We also developed a manufacturing and costing plan using CNC machining, tube bending, and robotic welding at a hypothetical 45,000-unit volume. This was a design exercise; no racks were manufactured.
